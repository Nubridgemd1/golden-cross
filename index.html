Meet your provider

Patricia Ayika, PMHNP‑BC

Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner

Patricia Ayika is a board‑certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP‑BC) caring for children, adolescents and adults. She provides comprehensive psychiatric evaluations, thoughtful medication management, and supportive therapy — always with a warm, collaborative approach that helps each person feel heard, respected and understood.

Care is available both in person at our Katy, TX office and through convenient, secure telehealth visits across Texas.

Our mission

Your Mental Health. Our Priority.

At Golden Cross Mental Health and Behavioral Center, our mission is to provide compassionate, accessible, and evidence‑based mental health care that empowers individuals and families to heal, grow and thrive. We meet every person with dignity and respect, walking alongside them on the path to lasting well‑being.
